About Me

I'm a UX researcher with experience leading end-to-end research for complex enterprise and fulfillment systems at Amazon, including internal tools used across large-scale operational environments.

I specialize in mixed methods research, combining qualitative approaches like interviews, usability testing, and design sprints with quantitative methods such as surveys, behavioral data, and product metrics. I don't just want to understand what users are doing — I want to understand why.

My background in psychology informs my research methodology, which is centered on curiosity and empathy. I'm driven by the belief that the best tools are the ones that genuinely serve the people who use them. I help teams make evidence-based decisions grounded in real user insights.

Outside of work, you'll find me hiking and exploring the outdoors with my camera in hand — I love using photography as a way to see the world from new perspectives. I'm also a big foodie who's always on the hunt for the next great meal or hidden gem restaurant.
